Privacy information

Yammer Devil Uploader and Darren’s Dashboard are internal tools used by their operator to manage the operator’s own eBay business.

Seller connection

Seller authorisation is requested on eBay. This service does not ask for or store your eBay password. It stores encrypted access and refresh tokens to maintain the authorised connection. Application credentials are stored as protected hosting secrets. The current connection requests read-only order-fulfilment access; order importing is not enabled by this setup step.

Account-deletion notices

The receiver checks eBay’s notification signature and stores the minimum identifiers supplied by eBay in an encrypted processing queue. Repeated deliveries are deduplicated. When processing is acknowledged, the queued identifiers are removed and a notification identifier and processing timestamp remain to prevent reprocessing. Dashboard’s local deletion processor is still being connected; receipt alone does not complete deletion from local records.

Use and hosting

Data handled by this integration is used for the operator’s business tools and connection security, not advertising or sale to third parties. The service is hosted using OpenAI Sites on Cloudflare infrastructure. Buyer data is not displayed on public pages. Access to queued identifiers requires separate authentication.

Retention and requests

There is no single retention period applied to every record. Buyer-data importing and its retention rules must be configured before live order imports are enabled. Necessary business-record retention and erasure requests are assessed according to the record and purpose. For questions about an order or personal information, contact the seller through eBay Messages.
