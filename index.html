Privacy information

Yammer Devil Uploader and Darren’s Dashboard are internal tools used by their operator to manage the operator’s own eBay business.

Seller connection and security

Seller authorisation takes place on eBay. This service does not ask for or store the seller’s eBay password. Encrypted access and refresh tokens maintain the authorised connection. Application credentials are protected hosting secrets. The connection requests order-read, messaging and finance permissions. The Windows app uses a separate, Windows-protected connection credential.

Orders and fulfilment

Dashboard displays orders placed in the last 90 days. Information used includes sale and delivery dates, buyer username, item title, quantity, selected variations, buyer notes, and explicitly labelled personalisation information where available. These details support the operator’s order fulfilment.

Live order rows remain in memory; the integration does not create a persistent live-order cache. Manual status choices and protected matching metadata are saved locally so they survive refreshes. Existing downloaded reports, earlier imported snapshots and separately retained exports may exist independently of the live view.

Messages, returns and refunds

eBay bundles reading, sending and managing messages into one messaging permission. This integration reads unread counts from active conversations; it does not send messages or change their read status. Message text is not stored.

The service reads summary counts of open returns and seller actions due or overdue. It also counts refund records associated with orders placed in the last 90 days. A refund record can be pending or failed and does not, by itself, mean that money has been paid. The return-summary feature does not accept or reject returns or issue refunds. Its buttons open the relevant eBay pages for the operator.

Listings and automatic stock control

The service reads active listing quantities and individual variation details. Aggregate stock counts are cached by the hosted service. If the operator enables automatic stock control, unambiguously identified active fixed-price variations with a known available quantity below five, including zero, are topped up to five. Quantities already at five or above are left alone; existing prices are preserved. Unknown or ambiguous variations require review.

This control checks every five minutes while Dashboard is running and processes updates in bounded batches. The operator can switch it off from the Low stock tile.

Finance summaries

Finance features read the latest payout amount, date and status, together with available, processing, held and total funds. They do not move money or change payout settings. Requests are digitally signed using a signing key stored encrypted by the hosted service. Bank-account details and full payout records are not sent to Dashboard. The integration does not persist raw finance responses as a finance cache.

Account-deletion notices

The receiver verifies eBay’s notification signature and stores the minimum identifiers supplied by eBay in an encrypted processing queue. Repeated deliveries are deduplicated. Dashboard processes matched notices before importing more orders: it removes matching app-managed buyer rows, matching manual status associations, and matching entries from its known imported snapshots and backups.

After successful processing, queued identifiers are removed. A notification identifier and processing timestamp remain to prevent duplicate processing, and protected buyer-matching markers prevent reimport. Receipt of a notice alone does not complete local processing. Original downloads, manual exports and unrelated business records require their own retention and deletion review.

Use and hosting

Information handled by this integration is used for the operator’s business tools, fulfilment and connection security, not advertising or sale to third parties. The integration is hosted using OpenAI Sites on Cloudflare infrastructure; this public policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. Buyer data, credentials and financial figures are not displayed on public pages. Access to the feed, summaries and deletion queue requires separate authentication.

Retention and contact

There is no single retention period applied to every record by this integration. Necessary business-record retention and erasure requests are assessed according to the record and purpose. For questions about an order or personal information, contact the seller through eBay Messages.
